Public interest and participation are essential for a successful feasibility study. Possible engagement strategies can include organizational outreach, meetings, and workshops to best engage the public and measure support.


Sample Questions: 
1. How would the creation of the Eastern Shore of Virginia National Heritage Area affect the communities and/or resources within the designated area, negatively or positively? 
2. Do you have comments or concerns about the suggested preliminary themes for the proposed Eastern Shore of Virginia National Heritage Area? 
3. Do you have suggestions for additional themes or subthemes that tell the nationally significant stories of the Eastern Shore of Virginia region? If so, how are these themes represented (resources, traditions, customs, or beliefs) in the proposed Eastern Shore of Virginia National Heritage Area?
